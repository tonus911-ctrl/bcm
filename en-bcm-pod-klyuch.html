Turnkey business continuity system

We start with an honest audit of what already exists and within 1-2 months bring it to a working system: plans people use, people who know their roles, risks in money and measured recovery time. Delivered in Russia, in English, for teams that report to a foreign parent, an insurer or a group auditor.

What companies come to us with

Three conversations that usually start the work.

  • “We have plans. They are in a folder with IT”The folder was opened for the audit. It does not say who declares a crisis, who calls clients and how to work without the ERP.
  • “The board asked what a day of downtime costs. We had no answer”There is a feeling and an insurance policy. Nobody has counted the numbers by process, people and systems.
  • “We are moving to Russian hardware and software. What if the new system goes down?”The migration will touch production, while the rollback plan and the way to work on the day of a failure are still in people’s heads.

How the work runs: seven steps

Three stages: understand, test, fix. Every step ends with a document you keep. The whole programme takes 1-2 months; the quote is ready within two working days.

  1. 01UnderstandWe choose with you two scenarios that genuinely threaten the companyBy default, a ransomware attack and a fire that closes a site. For a trading house it may be the failure of a cornerstone contract, for a family business the departure of key executives, for a manufacturer the loss of a supplier.
  2. 02We count the company’s economicsHow much the company earns and from what, where the financing comes from, where clients, suppliers and contractors are concentrated, which single points of failure and bottlenecks exist.
  3. 03We take apart the business model: how revenue is made and what would stop itHow revenue turns into EBITDA and net profit, and at which link a disruption cuts into profit. Without this step a plan protects the wrong processes.
  4. 04We talk to the people who make the moneyInterviews with key employees: who actually holds the process, how revenue is created and by which rules the company works today.
  5. 05TestWe test the plan in a stress test: two days in the meeting roomA tabletop “day X” scenario: CRM or ERP goes dark, a warehouse or a workshop is closed. Systems keep running as usual, while the decisions, gaps and timings of the day are real. How the check runs →
  6. 06FixWe build the recovery scheme together with your teamWho does what, in which hour and with which authority. People carry out plans they helped write.
  7. 07We put metrics in place and close the gaps we foundEverything fixable without investment we fix together right away: plans, fallback registers and channels, contract wording, offline copies. What needs a budget gets a risk-economics case, and you keep a 90-day plan.

One fix as an example

The programme is made of fixes like this: most of them cost nothing and are closed during the work.

  • The problem.The client register lives in the CRM, and that is exactly what ransomware encrypts first.
  • The fix.During the programme we export the register to a simple offline file kept away from the office and set up a fallback channel: a Telegram contact for every key client, with message templates ready.
  • Cost: zero.The effect on day X: sales keep talking to clients while IT recovers.

Frequently asked questions

We are a critical infrastructure operator moving to Russian hardware and software. Is this a separate project?

No. It is one of the continuity scenarios, and it fits into the same tools: the impact analysis gives the migration window, the risk register gains the migration risks, the plan gets a rollback section, and exercises rehearse a failure of the new system. Security tools and categorisation stay with specialist integrators and your own commission.

Does the price include the fixes?

Everything fixable without capital spend is fixed together during the programme: plans, registers, fallback channels, contract wording, drills. Measures that need investment come with a risk-economics case proving they pay back; the decision stays yours.

Will you really switch off our systems?

No. The stress test is a tabletop exercise: we simulate the loss of CRM and ERP or the closure of a site in a structured session. Production runs as usual, and the decisions, gaps and timings the day produces are real.

Our biggest risk is neither ransomware nor fire. Does the programme fit?

Yes. The two default scenarios are a starting point. For a trading house it may be the failure of a cornerstone contract, for a family business the departure of key executives, for a manufacturer the loss of a key supplier. We agree the two scenarios that genuinely threaten you at the start.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes, the scope adapts to the size of the company and its budget.

How is this different from the risk-place.org programme?

Same method, different market. This edition is delivered in Russia and priced in roubles, and the scenarios and regulatory context are local. The risk-place.org edition is built for the Gulf.
