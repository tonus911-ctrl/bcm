How we get there

  1. Two scenarios, chosen with youBy default: a ransomware attack and a fire that closes a site. Depending on your industry we swap in what threatens you most — loss of a key supplier, departure of key executives, failure of a cornerstone contract.
  2. Facts and economicsHow much the company earns and from where; financing sources; concentration of clients, suppliers and contractors; technology and raw-material dependence; single points of failure and bottlenecks.
  3. Business model deep-diveHow revenue is formed, which costs create it, how it becomes EBITDA and net profit. Continuity planning that skips this step protects the wrong things.
  4. Interviews with the people who make the moneyWe find who is actually key, how they generate revenue, and by which principles they work today.
  5. Then we break those principles — on paperA desk-based stress test: CRM or ERP goes dark, the warehouse or production block is closed by fire. Nothing is switched off in reality — but the company lives the day in the room, and we watch what it is capable of, in detail.
  6. Recovery, designed togetherWe build the recovery scheme with your team, not for them. People execute plans they helped write.
  7. Metrics — and the upliftWe implement survival metrics, then raise them: new plans, new contract clauses, offline backups, fallback registers and channels. Everything fixable for free we fix together immediately; what needs investment we prove through risk economics — and leave you with a working plan.
A small example from the programme

Your client registry lives in the CRM — which is exactly what ransomware encrypts. So during the programme we export it to a simple offline file, stored away from the office, and agree a fallback channel: a Telegram contact for every key client, collected in advance, with message templates ready. Cost: zero. Effect on day X: sales keep talking to clients while IT recovers. The programme is full of fixes like this.

Frequently asked questions

Does the price include the fixes?

Everything fixable without capital spend is fixed together during the programme — plans, registers, fallback channels, contract wording, drills. Measures that need investment come with a risk-economics case proving they pay back; the decision stays yours.

Will you really switch off our systems?

No. The stress test is desk-based: we simulate the loss of CRM and ERP or the closure of a site in a structured exercise. Production is never touched — but the decisions, gaps and timings the day produces are real.

Our biggest risk is neither ransomware nor fire. Does the programme fit?

Yes. The two default scenarios are a starting point. For a trading house it may be the failure of a cornerstone contract; for a family business, the departure of key executives; for a manufacturer, a key supplier. We agree the two scenarios that genuinely threaten you at the start.

How is this different from the risk-place.org programme?

Same method, different market. This edition is delivered in Russia and priced in roubles, and the scenarios and regulatory context are local. The risk-place.org edition is built for the Gulf.
