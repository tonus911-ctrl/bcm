Business crisis readiness checklist: 12 points

Check in 10 minutes how ready your business is for a disruption. There are 12 points. If the answer to most of them is “no”, there is work to do. This is the express version of the resilience assessment.

12-point checklist

Tick what is already done:

  • The 3–5 critical processes are identified: those the business cannot work without for a single day.
  • The cost of one day of downtime of a key process is calculated.
  • Recovery time objectives (RTO) and acceptable data loss (RPO) are set.
  • Points of risk concentration are found (a single server/supplier/person/channel).
  • There are short instructions for the first 2 hours after a disruption, on paper or in a messaging app.
  • People responsible for decisions in a crisis are appointed.
  • There are backups, and restoring them has been tested.
  • Isolated (offline) copies exist in case of ransomware.
  • There are backup suppliers and backup channels for reaching customers.
  • A financial cushion for the downtime period is calculated.
  • The team has run an exercise based on a disruption scenario.
  • The plan has been reviewed in the last 12 months.
How to read the result: 10–12 “yes” answers: high readiness; 6–9: medium, with gaps; 5 or fewer: the business is vulnerable, so start with critical processes and a plan for the first hours.

What to do next

Close the gaps in order of priority: first critical processes and instructions for the first hours, then redundancy and exercises. Details are in “How to write a BCP” and “Business impact analysis (BIA)”. For an accurate picture, take the free resilience assessment: 13 questions.

Frequently asked questions

How many “yes” answers mean the business is ready?

10–12 out of 12: high readiness. 6–9: medium, with gaps. 5 or fewer: high vulnerability, so start with the basic measures.

How is the checklist different from the assessment on this site?

The checklist is a quick self-check. The 13-question assessment gives your resilience level, the three main risks and the first steps, with the result shown on screen.

From the requirements of the standard to a working system

The assessment shows the gap between your documents and reality. Then the system is built either by your own team in the programme or by us as a turnkey project.
