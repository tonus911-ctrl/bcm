The cost of not seeing

The cure for blindness is an instrument that shows the state at all times.

  • ₽1.8M per day.A typical cost of one day down for a mid-size business — most companies never calculated theirs.
  • 40% never reopen.The share of small businesses that never resume operations after a major disruption (FEMA).
  • 26 hours instead of 4.Actual recovery time is often several times the target — discovered during the outage.

What you will see on the dashboard

Six questions a leader asks, answered on one screen.

  • How protected are we?Resilience index 0-100 with its trend: one number for the board conversation.
  • What fails first?Critical processes with traffic lights: readiness, RTO/RPO, owners.
  • Where are the thin threads?Single points of failure: one person, one supplier, one server, one AI service.
  • What does an hour of downtime cost?Financial exposure: cost of downtime and the recovery cushion.
  • Are the plans alive?BCP/DRP coverage, review dates, when exercises ran and what they revealed.
  • What changed?Quarterly trends: where things improved and where degradation started.

How the dashboard works

No heavy IT system involved: the dashboard is a single HTML file built by a small Python engine.

The engine runs entirely inside your perimeter — on the coordinator's laptop, a corporate server or your private cloud. Data never leaves the company.

  • History and trends.On every rebuild the engine stores a dated snapshot in a lightweight embedded database (SQLite) next to itself. So the dashboard shows the current state and the dynamics: what improved over the quarter and where degradation started.

How the dashboard differs from a BI report

Three differences that matter for a continuity system.

  • Methodology inside.A BI team spends months deciding what to measure. Here the continuity metrics are already selected, defined and linked — fill from day one.
  • No IT project needed.The start requires no integrations, servers or procurement: Excel plus a light engine inside your perimeter.
  • It stays alive after the project.Scheduled updates and traffic lights keep the continuity system from quietly dying.

Frequently asked questions

Will the data leave the company?

No. The engine and the data live entirely inside your perimeter — on your computer, server or private cloud. Not a single number goes outside.

We have no data for a dashboard. Where do we start?

With the Excel workbook: the methodology inside tells you what to collect. If there is no baseline data at all, we will gather it together in an express assessment — part of the Start level.

Who will update the data?

Your coordinator — 1-2 hours a month following the guidance. Or subscribe to the monthly service and we will handle it.

How long until the first dashboard?

Usually 2 weeks from the start: setup, form filling, first build and a results review.

Is this a certified monitoring system?

No. It is a management tool and a methodology — honestly, with no inflated promises.
