A day without the ledger: the decision, the counterparties and output

While the database is closed, the company runs the day with letters and reduced output. Below, who takes these decisions and what in them should already be written down.

Updated: 31 October 2026 · Author: Evgeny Telenkov
On this page

A day without the ledger system is a day of decisions, texts and reduced output. Who may promise a new date, what exactly goes to the supplier and the customer, what the company says if the event is asked about in public, and what share of throughput remains.

Who decides on that day

The right to promise a date to a customer and the right to stop shipping are written down before the attack. On a day without the database these two rights conflict: sales wants to keep the relationship, the site wants to avoid promising what it will not ship.

The priority is one, and it is set in advance. First, what the company can ship on the second path. Then an honest new date for the rest. An argument between two directors in a messenger reads to the customer as two different promises.

Suppliers, customers and the public account

The supplier receives a request to hold or move the next delivery of raw material, with the reason in one sentence and the hour when the company will confirm the schedule. The customer receives a new date only for the items the site has confirmed.

If the event is asked about from outside, the company states the fact that the system is closed, the consequence for shipping and the hour of the next message. It does not add a version of the culprit that it does not have.

  • One date in one letter. A second letter the same day does not change the date.
  • The raw-material supplier learns of the pause before a vehicle is already at the gate.
  • The public text matches the customer text on the facts.
  • A promise of full shipping on a day without the database stays out of the letters.

A cyber policy, where there is one, pays on its own terms and after its own documents. It does not choose which date to give the customer in that hour.

What the readiness check calculates

The ransomware readiness check turns this day into figures: what share of output is alive without the database, what the lost day costs, who writes to the counterparty, and which text already sits in the duty manager's folder.

Frequently asked questions

Whether to pay a ransom on that day

A decision to pay does not restore shipping in the same hour and does not replace the second path. A named circle takes it, under a rule written before the attack. That decision is not in the letter to the customer.

How to tell that the database can run the day again

By the share of output it again confirms with documents. Until a shipping document from the database matches what actually left, the day is still manual.

Is a separate text needed for the press

A short one is needed, and it is built from the same three sentences as the customer letter: what is closed, what that means for supply, when the next news will come.
