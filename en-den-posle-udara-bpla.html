The morning after a drone strike: who writes, and which date is allowed

The next morning the customer is given a single date for when shipments start again. Who is allowed to name that date is decided before morning. What is said in public matches the letter. The insurer does not choose the date.

Updated: 31 October 2026 · Author: Evgeny Telenkov

The expensive conversation after a strike on a site is not with a journalist. It is with the customer who asks when the vehicle is actually going to arrive.

The answer that morning has to be a single one. A single date in a single letter. A second letter the same day does not move that date. While the date is missing, each following day costs the company its ordinary day of downtime. In the published logistics account that day came to about 180,000 rubles. A new date during the day does not shrink that sum: it only pushes the promise further out.

Who is allowed to decide that morning

Before the event the roles are on paper, and each role has a name. Who stops the shift. Who is allowed to give the customer a new date, and within what limit. Who writes to the material supplier and the contractor. Who speaks if the event is asked about in public.

A name that appears for the first time that morning is already late. While people look for the person allowed to promise, vehicles stand, and the customer hears several different versions from several people.

What goes to the supplier, the customer and the public text

The material supplier needs to hear about the pause before a vehicle is already at a closed gate. The letter carries three plain things: shipment or intake has stopped, which site is closed, and when the next message comes.

The customer receives a single date and a plain explanation of which part of the order can still leave. The public text repeats the same facts. A cause the company does not yet have is not announced that morning. The fact of the stop, which flow has stopped, and the hour of the next message are enough.

  • To the supplier. The pause, which intake is closed, when to expect the next letter.
  • To the customer. A single date, and which part of the order is still moving.
  • In public. The same facts as in the customer letter, without a cause the company does not have.

Where the policy stops and the letter begins

Where a downtime policy exists, it pays on its own terms and after its own documents. It does not choose the date for the customer that morning. The company writes the letter, from a text prepared in advance.

The letters go out the same day. The payment, if it comes, comes later. Which share of output can still be promised is decided by the person whose name was written down before morning.

The roles, the texts and the cost of a day for a single site are written down on the drone-attack readiness check.

Frequently asked questions

Who may promise the customer a new date?

Only the person named before that day, and only within the limit written next to the name. Everyone else may report facts. They do not move the date.

What is said when the cause is still unclear?

That the stop happened, which flow stopped, and when the next message comes. A cause the company does not have is not announced.

Can the insurer write the customer letter?

No. The insurer pays after its documents. The company writes the letter.
