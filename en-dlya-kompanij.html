For companies

Four products for companies: a live picture of resilience for the board, a turnkey continuity system, training for the management team and a safety programme for the whole workforce. We deliver the result with our own hands.

01 · For leadership

Continuity and risk dashboard

One live picture of resilience for the CEO, the board and audit. The first screen is ready in two weeks; it runs inside your perimeter, and the data stays in the company.

Learn more →
  • Resilience index and the status of critical processes
  • Cost of a downtime day in your own numbers

02 · With an expert

Turnkey continuity system

From an audit of what exists to a working system in seven steps and 1-2 months, with a plan check in a stress test. It also fits critical infrastructure operators moving to Russian hardware and software.

Learn more →
  • Plans the duty shift actually uses

  • People who know their roles, and measured recovery time
03 · For the team

Corporate continuity training

Two days with your management team on your own processes, with a crisis exercise at the end. On site or by video, up to eight people.

Learn more →
  • Draft plans for your own processes
  • A crisis exercise with a debrief
  • Templates, first-day checklists and personal certificates
04 · For the whole workforce

SAFE: safety culture programme

A corporate programme for workers, foremen and everyone who works near hazards every day. Taken on a phone with nothing to install, from 5,000 ₽ per person in a group of 50 or more.
