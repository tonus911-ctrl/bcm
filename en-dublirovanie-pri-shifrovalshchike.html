Duplication and separation: a second path for ransomware

A second way to take an order and ship commodity goods makes sense only where ransomware does not reach. Below are four parts of that path and their limit.

Updated: 31 October 2026 · Author: Evgeny Telenkov
On this page

Duplication under ransomware is a second way to perform a step of the day when the first system is closed. Separation is the decision to keep that second way where the same attack does not reach: another site, another path, an export the attack can no longer change.

What exactly is duplicated

The company does not duplicate everything. It duplicates the steps without which a day of shipping equals zero: taking the order, knowing the commodity balance, the right to release a vehicle, and the contact of the person who confirms a date to the customer.

ElementWhere the pair sitsWhat the pair does not promise
The data copyA path the live attack does not rewriteAn instant return of the whole database
Taking the orderA form and a person allowed to promise a dateThe full history of discounts
The balanceAn export of commodity items to other storageThe address of every bin
ShippingAn exit document agreed in advanceAny route and any window
  • A pair on the same network as the working database falls with it.
  • An export nobody has opened is not readable on the day of the attack.
  • The right to promise a date to a customer is duplicated by job, as is the right to stop shipping.
  • The rehearsal of the second path is short: one order, one shipment, one new date.

The limit of the second path

The second path holds the commodity flow and an honest conversation about the date. It does not produce a unique specification and it does not replace the ledger the company is obliged to restore. Its job is to let the restoration proceed while whatever can still ship, ships.

Separation without a rehearsal remains a diagram. A rehearsal without separation tests the path the attack will close first.

How this is tested on a company

The ransomware readiness check looks at whether there is a second way for each step that feeds the day, whether it sits outside the working database, and whether it has been raised on at least one practice order.

Frequently asked questions

Does a copy on the same server count as a duplicate

Not for the hour of the attack. If ransomware reached the working database along this network, a neighbouring copy on the same path is the same kind of target.

What to rehearse first

One order from a form and one shipment of a commodity item from an exported balance, with a letter to the customer about the date. That is the day the company wants to keep.

Should a unique recipe be duplicated on paper

It is kept apart from the working database, in a place with limited access. A paper copy on the shop floor that anyone can photograph does not do the job of separation.
