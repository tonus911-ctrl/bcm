Who it is for

  • Operators, foremen, drivers, warehouse staff — everyone who spends the day next to real hazards.
  • The occupational safety specialist — who needs to reach hundreds of people and see who genuinely went through the material.
  • The site manager — who is accountable for people going home in one piece, and has to prove it.

The language is built for a mass audience: short sentences, real examples, paired «how it happens» and «how it should be».

The programme — 5 modules, 30 chapters

Each chapter is about four minutes of reading, with one or two diagrams and a workplace situation to think through.

01

What everyone needs to know: your role, rights and duties · 6 chapters

Where personal safety at work begins. Who is responsible for what, what the employer must provide before work starts, what depends on you alone, and how the right to stop unsafe work actually works.

02

Hazards and risks: how to see and assess them · 7 chapters

The difference between a hazard and a risk, in plain examples. A one-minute risk assessment, working at height and with electricity, machinery and vehicles, slow-acting harm, a five-minute workplace check and the hierarchy of controls.

03

Safety culture: prevention instead of reaction · 6 chapters

Why small things matter more than after-the-fact reviews. The incident pyramid, how to report a hazard so that it is heard, the stop card, a blame-free case review, and what to do when a hazard is not removed.

04

Responding to an incident: the first hour · 6 chapters

The sequence that saves two people. The first minute, what must never be done, the limits of first aid, what happens after an accident, fire and near-miss events, and your rights during an investigation.

05

Industry specifics · 5 chapters

Where irreversible harm happens most often, three severe scenarios and the barriers against them, high-risk work and permits, contractors working alongside you, and a personal shift checklist. Released in industry editions.

Frequent questions

How long does the programme take?

About two hours of reading. Participants go at their own pace, from a phone or a computer, and can return to any chapter.

Does anything need to be installed?

No. Each participant gets a personal link and opens the programme in a browser. No password is needed, progress is saved automatically.

How do we see who has completed it?

The management view shows every participant: chapters read, module tests passed, final check completed. You get a notification when someone finishes.

What kind of certificate does an employee receive?

A personal certificate named after the programme itself — «Safety culture and workplace risk assessment» — with a unique number. It is a record of preparation, not a state accreditation, and it does not replace the documents a company issues under occupational safety rules. What it confirms is the thing that matters: the person completed the programme and demonstrated, on the knowledge check, that they can see hazards around them, assess them and act during an incident.

Can the content be adapted to our industry?

Yes. Module five is released in industry editions — metals, oil and gas, warehousing and logistics, office. The other modules are common to all.

Can one person take the programme without a company?

Yes. Individual access costs 9,900 ₽ and the content is identical. See the section take the programme on your own.

How do we pay?

By invoice from your company. Write to info@risk-place.ru and we will send an invoice and a contract.
