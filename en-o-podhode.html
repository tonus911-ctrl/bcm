The ERGP Circle

Passing ERGP admits you to the Circle, a private community of certified practitioners coordinated by the founders. One Circle for the Russian, English and Arabic editions of the programme. Small today, it grows with every certificate issued.

Membership is earned once, at the examination, and carries three things with it.

Knowledge
Ahead of the market
  • A quarterly Circle session: one real case, dissected with the founders and the examiners
  • New modules and regulatory readings reach members before publication
  • Working templates from live engagements
People
A directory you can call
  • A private member directory with specialisation and contacts, open to members only
  • Direct access to the examiners and to each other
  • Peers who solve the same problem in the next industry
Opportunities
Called upon for real work
  • We assign Circle members to client engagements: audits, dashboards, continuity systems
  • Members with standing become examiners and co-authors of modules
  • Your name in the public register, verifiable by any employer

How the practice works

We came to resilience from inside organisations.

The plans we write for clients we first executed ourselves, in an industrial group, in an international bank, under a regulator's inspection. Our plans are short and written for the worst day.

We count risk in money.

A conversation about the cost of a downtime day ends with a decision. In every engagement a model appears where a disruption reaches revenue and profit.

We build the system together with the client's team.

We assemble the recovery scheme in working sessions with those who will act, and leave the company a shared language along with the documents.

The practice covers industry, finance, insurance, transport, IT and retail; ISO 22301 and the Russian GOST R profile; preparing companies for audits and for the conversation with the regulator. More than eight hundred professionals have completed our programmes. The methodology is published openly in Materials and the glossary.
