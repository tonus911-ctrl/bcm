The ERGP Circle

Passing ERGP admits you to the Circle, the professional community of certified practitioners coordinated by the founders. There is one Circle for the Russian, English and Arabic editions of the programme. It is small today and grows with every certificate issued.

Membership is granted once, at the examination, and it gives you three things.

  • Knowledge ahead of the market.A quarterly Circle session: one real case, analysed with the founders and the examiners. New modules and analyses of regulatory requirements reach members before publication. Working templates from live projects.
  • People you can call.A closed member directory with specialisations and contacts, available only inside the Circle. Direct access to the examiners and to each other. Peers who solve the same problem in a neighbouring industry.
  • You are invited to real projects.We assign Circle members to company projects: audits, dashboards, building a continuity system. Long-standing members become examiners and co-authors of modules. Your name is in the public register, which any employer can check.
