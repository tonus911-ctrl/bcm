After two days the team leaves with documents started on your data and a shared language.

The programme: six modules in two days

Every module ends with hands-on work on your company’s material.

At the end the team plays out a crisis scenario in a tabletop exercise.

  1. 01Foundations of business continuity management (BCM)Business continuity and operational resilience, ISO 22301 and national standards, regulatory expectations, including central bank requirements for financial organisations. Roles, accountability and the place of continuity in enterprise risk management.
  2. 02Business impact analysis (BIA) and risk assessmentThe critical process register, recovery time and recovery point objectives (RTO and RPO), pricing an hour and a day of downtime. Hunting single points of failure across IT, people, suppliers and sites. What to protect first.
  3. 03The business continuity plan (BCP) and disaster recovery plan (DRP)The structure of a plan people actually use. Recovery and redundancy strategies, manual workarounds for critical operations, backups and IT recovery requirements.
  4. 04Crisis managementThe crisis team, its authority and activation. First-24-hours checklists and crisis communications: what to tell staff, clients and the press. Classic failures dissected on real cases: ransomware attacks, IT outages, supplier losses.
  5. 05Cyber resilienceThe ransomware scenario and the first hours of response. 3-2-1 backups, recovery after an attack, connecting information security with continuity.
  6. 06Exercises, metrics and keeping the system aliveExercise types, including tabletop cyber drills. Key resilience metrics and the executive dashboard (live demo →), auditing the system and keeping it in working order.

Frequently asked questions

How long is it and in what format?

Two days, six modules. On site at your company or live by video, which suits distributed teams. We pick the dates together; weekdays and weekends both work.

How many people are in a group?

Up to eight participants. For a larger group we clarify the goals and either split it or adapt the cases so everyone gets hands-on work.

How is this different from the ERGP online programme?

Here the work runs on your company’s material and in dialogue: we go through your processes, draft plans and play out a scenario. If you need a structured self-paced programme with a knowledge check and a certificate, that is ERGP.

Can the programme be adapted to our industry?

Yes, and it is included in the price. Cases, exercises and focus are adapted to your industry, regulatory requirements and IT landscape.

What do participants get at the end?

A knowledge check and a personal certificate of completion of the programme, plus working materials: templates, first-24-hours checklists and a crisis communications structure.

How do we pay?

By invoice to your organisation. Write to info@risk-place.ru and we will send the programme, an invoice and a contract.
