Frequently asked questions

What is ERGP?

ERGP stands for Enterprise Resilience Governance Professional, an online course and certification in business resilience governance. The name is international because the programme is published in three languages and is also released for the international market.

How long does the course take?

You work through the ERGP programme at your own pace and can start any day. Access to the materials is open for 6 months from the date of payment. That is more than enough to complete all six modules, answer the self-check questions and return to the chapters you need before the final assignment.

What certificate will I get?

A personal ERGP certificate with a unique number. It is a risk-place certificate of completion of the programme, not a state document. Its value lies in the knowledge check behind it: six tests and a review of the final assignment. Authenticity is verified by number.

How does ERGP differ from webinars and one-day courses?

It is a systematic programme with a knowledge check: a self-check after each part, a test after each module and, at the end, a final assignment reviewed in an online meeting.

How does the in-person group differ from the online course?

The programme, the tests and the certificate are the same. The in-person group adds days in Moscow on 5–13 December 2026: a live review of your documents and of your final assignment in the group. The online course is included in the in-person format.

Can we take it as a team?

Yes, there is group access for teams. Write to us and we will discuss the format and terms.

How can an organisation pay?

By invoice. Write to info@risk-place.ru and we will send an invoice for payment by your organisation.

What languages is the programme published in?

Russian, English and Arabic. The ERGP course was created for the international market and is also published for the Gulf countries.
