Why it is worth acting now

An attack takes hours, and preparing for it takes five weeks. Here is what 2026 research says.

  • Ransomware is on the rise.In the first quarter of 2026 the number of ransomware attacks on Russian companies grew by 20%, and the number of incidents in industry and transport by 24%.Kaspersky, 3 June 2026
  • An attack stops the whole business, well beyond one IT system.In 98% of ransomware and wiper attacks, core business processes were disrupted. Losses at large companies ranged from RUB 50 m to RUB 1.5 bn.Positive Technologies, Vedomosti, 14 October 2026
  • A backup is no guarantee of recovery.Attackers get to the backups too. Almost half of attacks come with a data leak, and since 30 May 2025 a repeat leak of personal data can bring a turnover-based fine.Vedomosti, 14 October 2026
  • What you can do this week.Check that one backup copy is kept offline, print out the contacts of key customers and suppliers, and agree a backup communication channel for management.

Frequently asked questions

Will you hack into our systems?

No. The readiness check looks at how the company would get through an attack: processes, backups, management decisions. If you need external security testing, a specialist partner runs the penetration test and the simulated attack under a separate contract and NDA.

Will you shut down our ERP for the check?

No. We test recovery on a test environment or in an agreed window. Your IT team carries out every action in the systems; we observe and time it.

We have an information security team and backups. Why do we need a check?

The information security team protects against an attack. The check answers a different question the owner has: what happens if the protection fails. How sales and the warehouse work without systems, how long data recovery really takes and what each day of downtime costs.

What information do you need, and who sees it?

A list of processes and systems, your backup procedure, key contracts with customers and suppliers. Everything is shared under a non-disclosure agreement, and the report stays with you.

Can we just talk the situation through first?

Yes. The first 30-minute Zoom call commits you to nothing: we look at your situation and tell you whether you need the check and in what scope.

How is the check different from Turnkey BCM?

The check looks at one scenario in five weeks and ends with findings for the owner. Turnkey BCM builds a continuity system for the whole company: plans, roles, exercises, metrics. Half the cost of the check is credited if you move on to the programme.

Can this be combined with the drone strike check?

Yes. The two checks together cost RUB 1,690,000 instead of RUB 1,980,000, excluding taxes, with one set of findings for the owner.
